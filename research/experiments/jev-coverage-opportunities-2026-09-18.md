# Jev coverage opportunities — 2026-09-18

**Historical proposal, superseded September 19, 2026.** The subsequent [procurement pilot](procurement-review-2026-09-19.md) did not establish alpha, and the [project is paused](../../PLAN.md). The recommendations below are preserved as the original research record; they do not authorize further searches or experiments.

**Research decision:** investigate local procurement records first, with broad semantic changes in corporate filings as the backup. Industrial disruptions rank third. This is a judgment about feasibility and fit, not a measured ranking of profitable strategies. No prices, paid model/API calls, or trades were used in this research.

The relevant hypothesis is that cheap, fast semantic processing expands useful coverage across fragmented original sources. It does not imply superior financial judgment. A method must beat simple extraction and a conventional model, and any return must survive source availability, realistic entry delays, costs and existing expectations. Our small, long-only account favors information that remains useful for days over a headline-speed race.

**1. Local procurement and infrastructure decisions.** Buyer agendas, staff recommendations, bid tabs and minutes can identify contractor work before an issuer bundles it into investor news. Jev could distinguish bidder from winner; recommendation from approval; new work from renewal; incremental commitment from total budget; and a listed subsidiary from an unrelated namesake. Arithmetic, document matching and verified ownership should remain deterministic.

Two examples justify investigating the source, not buying the stocks:

- **Port Tampa Bay / Orion, June 2025:** the June 17 [official agenda](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Public/Agenda/1448?meeting=665855) displays June 12, 11:08 AM beside the Berth 301 attachment. The [packet](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Public/DownloadAgenda/1448?meeting=665855), PDF page 25, recommends Orion Marine Construction for **$18.247 million**, subject to counsel review. Its $22 million project authorization also includes alternatives and contingency. June 17 [minutes](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Public/DownloadAgenda/1448?meeting=665856) record approval; Orion's [June 19 announcement](https://www.oriongroupholdingsinc.com/investors/press-release/2025/19-06-2025-120101602) includes the award. Bids arrived May 29, potentially revealing the winner earlier.
- **TxDOT / Orion, January–February 2025:** the January 30 [award exhibit](https://www.txdot.gov/content/dam/docs/commission/2025/0130/4a.pdf), PDF page 16, identifies Orion Construction LLC and **$113,740,588.46** for the Lake Waco bridge. [Minutes](https://www.txdot.gov/content/dam/docs/commission/2025/0130/minutes.pdf) record unanimous approval, but also earlier public bid openings on January 7–8. Orion's [February 10 release](https://www.oriongroupholdingsinc.com/investors/press-release/2025/10-02-2025-120211765) announces the contract and roughly two-year construction period. TxDOT describes an [eight-day advance agenda schedule](https://www.txdot.gov/about/leadership/texas-transportation-commission/meeting-dates-agendas.html).

These are two agencies but **one issuer**. Current attachment timestamps and meeting dates do not independently prove first public availability or unchanged historical bytes. Neither example exhausts earlier procurement notices, trade reporting or investor expectations. Approval can be new legal status without new economic information. Contract revenue also does not establish margin or earnings surprise.

**Source architecture.** Start with an explicit issuer/subsidiary/buyer map and a registry of official public portals. [Legistar's documented API](https://webapi.legistar.com/Home/Examples) supports public GETs, pagination, events, matters, attachments and histories; some clients require tokens. A live Seattle read succeeded and exposed a future meeting's agenda PDF; NYC returned an explicit token-required denial, with no bypass attempted. [Event metadata](https://webapi.legistar.com/Help/Api/GET-v1-Client-Events) includes last-published timestamps, not guaranteed first publication. Current action outcomes can contain later information.

[BoardBook](https://www.boardbook.org/faq) supplies public agenda pages and downloadable packets; no unrestricted documented bulk API was established. [CivicClerk's public portal](https://www.civicplus.help/meetings-select/docs/embed-or-link-to-the-public-portal) supplies meeting documents, while API access must be verified per product and jurisdiction. Save each observed version, URL, retrieval time and byte hash. Separate recommendation, vote, execution and later amendment. Historical records support discovery; uncertain historical clocks require prospective capture before executable claims.

**2. Broad filing changes, backup.** Compare economically meaningful changes across many 10-K/10-Q sections: customer concentration, contracts, litigation, financing and risk descriptions. Jev's potential contribution is affordable semantic interpretation of many diffs, beyond lexical similarity. [Lazy Prices](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25084) found historical return predictability using **1995–2014** filings, with prominent results from buying non-changers and shorting changers. This is neither evidence of current alpha nor a direct fit for our long-only account. Reliable dated document access and a strong lexical baseline remain prerequisites.

**3. Industrial disruptions.** [TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/airquality/emission-events/eventreporting) offers narrative incident records, exports and an hourly RSS feed. Jev could classify facility, process, duration and plausible affected supply chains. Reporting delay, operational ambiguity and uncertain listed competitor benefits weaken the long-only thesis. An emissions incident is not automatically a material production outage.

Clinical-registry changes, job postings and federal awards rank lower for immediate work. [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/data-api/api) refreshes on weekdays, generally by 9 AM ET; obvious changes are already structured, while interpreting amendments requires expertise. [Greenhouse](https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html) public GET access worked, returning 453 current Datadog postings, but current postings do not supply a complete historical hiring archive. [SAM's Contract Awards API](https://open.gsa.gov/api/contract-awards/) has a 90-day public DoD reveal delay and restrictive default personal-key limits. Separate agency announcements can appear sooner. USAspending access worked, but cumulative award amounts must not be mistaken for incremental new obligations.

**Bounded next census.** Freeze 10–20 relevant buyer portals, a small listed-contractor universe and a six-month window before inspecting prices. Choose the roster from documented buyer exposure and access, not from successful announcement examples; the two Orion cases above remain discovery cases outside confirmatory evaluation. Retain every procurement item, including private winners, immaterial contracts, failures and duplicates. Audit earlier public disclosures, available clocks, ownership/JV shares, incremental value and novelty. Count how many independent issuers and economically material events remain actionable after a next-session delay. Stop if the source supply or chronology cannot support a credible study.

Compare rules, Jev and a conventional model on identical inputs at equal coverage; separately compare useful recall under an equal total review budget, including retrieval, OCR and human review. Measure actual OpenRouter throughput and errors; provider marketing benchmarks do not establish our pipeline's speed. If a conventional pipeline can affordably cover the same corpus and find the same events, Jev has not opened a new opportunity. Escalate ambiguous cases. Only after a source/feature freeze should returns be tested against sector/size controls, simple award-value rules and an issuer-announcement baseline, using chronological holdouts and prospective confirmation. This census is a proposal, not an already-running monitor or evidence of profit.
