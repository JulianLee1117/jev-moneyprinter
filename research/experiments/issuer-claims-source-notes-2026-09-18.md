## What this means for Jev

A useful broad experiment must pair claimant identity with claim period, accounting recognition, cash receipt, customer pass-through, successor ownership and subsequent-event paragraphs. These examples show why a plausible gross refund extraction is insufficient. Freeze each as-of signal and compare dense Jev judgments with matched baselines before any price outcomes.

MBC’s remaining IEEPA administrative rights are a bounded source lead, not an alpha recommendation. Refresh intervening filings and CBP/court state before treating the arithmetic remainder as outstanding. LPX remains a local no-timed-catalyst example; UFPI must not be labeled negative merely because it was selected as an industry control.
