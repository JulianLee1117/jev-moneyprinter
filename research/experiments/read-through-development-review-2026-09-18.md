# Semiconductor composition experiment: development result

Research date: September 18, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. This is an implementation and development-test record. **No trading returns, profitable alpha, or unique Jev advantage have been established.**

The research objective is making money from Jev's decision capabilities; its inference-cost advantage alone does not satisfy that objective. The active hypothesis is that interpreting changes in semiconductor spending composition can reveal supplier-demand implications that a total-capex headline misses. Code must distinguish new guidance from repeated guidance, buildings from tools, delivery deferrals from demand changes, and net funding from equipment purchases. These distinctions can matter financially; whether stock prices leave an opportunity after the decision remains untested.

Among three source scouts, this family has the clearest repeatable source population for the next test. Odd-lot tenders have a real small-account mechanism, but a conventional offer extractor may already capture it and dollar opportunities are limited. Industrial nitrogen outages have useful product-level constraints, but spare capacity, contract repricing and deliverable incremental volume remain substantial gaps. Those alternatives are preserved in their source-scout reports; this choice is provisional, not a measured ranking of profitability.

## What actually ran

Three purposely chosen pre-2025 source pairs supplied 18 reviewed interpretation questions: TSMC's 2022 spending cut; Micron's 2023 construction/equipment split and repeated guidance; Intel's 2022 gross-versus-net capex. All six model requests completed and passed their transport/schema/serving-model checks. The same original evidence and atomic questions went to pinned Jev and `openai/gpt-5.4-mini`; the conventional model returned compact categories without being forced to invent probability arrays.

Jev matched **17/18** frozen agent-reviewed labels; the conventional control matched **16/18**. These are three selected development episodes, not 18 independent financial observations or a representative accuracy benchmark. Jev's sole disagreement was an `unknown` on whether a net cash-spending reduction necessarily equals an order reduction. The reviewed `no` rejects that universal implication because offsets affect the net measure; `unknown` is more defensible when interpreted as the actual order change. This remaining wording distinction limits any accuracy comparison. The original label and score are retained.

The conventional model incorrectly treated the Micron packet as establishing a comparable prior FY2024 total-dollar budget revision, and treated higher total spending alone as establishing a new positive WFE-demand revision. Its separate WFE-direction answer was correct, so deterministic condition logic still rejected the misleading positive-demand path. WFE remaining down year over year does not establish that its exact amount was unchanged since the prior announcement. HBM assembly/test spending is a separate channel, not evidence for a blanket aggregate WFE revision.

Four source-interpretation routes were registered before inference. Jev matched three reference outcomes and left the fourth unresolved; the conventional control matched all four. **Native confidence routing changed zero routes relative to categorical Jev answers.** A small atomic-score lead therefore did not translate into a demonstrated decision advantage. These routes are evidence tests, not trade recommendations. No probabilities were multiplied into joint causal confidence or trade-win probabilities.

Reported model expense for the entire new phase was $0.007499154 against a $2 accounting cap, with no failed attempts or retries. This is recorded for accounting only; it does not satisfy the project's success criterion. Sixteen Jev answers had chosen-category probability at least 0.80, and fifteen matched the frozen labels; that tiny selected sample does not establish calibration.

## Concrete next experiment

The full event census is now frozen in `research/experiments/read-through-event-census.v1.json`: **13 scheduled TSMC/Micron releases**, January 2025 through July 2026, selected before inspecting event returns. Seven are TSMC and six are Micron. The Micron December 2024 predecessor is captured separately. Source indexes and displayed release dates are verified; complete original signal packets, intervening guidance reconciliation and dated supplier exposure are not yet finished. The census explicitly prohibits price collection until all arm predictions are frozen.

The financial protocol fixes a delayed, five-session, long-only LRCX diagnostic with SOXX comparison, whole-share $1,000 capital, overlapping-position accounting, bid/ask execution proxies and prespecified slippage. It compares numeric rules, conventional-model decisions, native Jev and categorical Jev, plus always-long and cash. It retains missing and unchanged events. Thirteen clustered events can debug or reject a hypothesis; they cannot establish reliable profitability. The [customer-link literature](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Economic-Links-and-Predictable-Returns) motivates the mechanism but does not demonstrate a current edge.

A separate prospective prior is already archived for Micron's [September 30 earnings call](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Technology-to-Report-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-Results-on-September-30-2026/default.aspx): sixteen original documents and twenty verified evidence spans. Annual capex, multiyear manufacturing commitments, research-lab plans and supplier financing are kept separate. June earnings Q&A, the August KeyBanc webcast and new disclosures before September 30 still need reconciliation. No automatic monitoring or orders were created.

TypeSafe's [native probability interface](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence) makes uncertainty directly usable by code. Its [documented limitations](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13) support keeping arithmetic and dependent reasoning outside the model. Neither feature establishes exclusive information or better financial predictions. The continuation criterion is incremental useful economic information and ultimately post-decision net returns; cheaper inference, more questions and a favorable selected-example score do not qualify by themselves.
