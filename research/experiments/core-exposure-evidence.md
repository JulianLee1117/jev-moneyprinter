# CORE issuer exposure evidence, 18 September 2026

The [candidate map](core-exposure-map.v1.json) now has six dated versions: Nucor and Steel Dynamics, each from FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025 filings. It establishes product relevance, not the sign or size of a stock-price response. No event return series was inspected to construct it.

Both issuers have domestic galvanized-sheet operations. Nucor's Mexican galvanized joint venture, sheet contracts and downstream businesses create additional channels. Steel Dynamics combines domestic coating, fabrication, processing, distribution and export customers. These are reasons to ask separate questions about each channel; none alone establishes a positive or negative consolidated earnings effect. The quantitative fields retain their actual denominators. Sheet revenue is broader than CORE, capacity is not production, and neither is a measure of earnings at risk.

Nucor's annual evidence comes from [FY2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/73309/000095017024021195/nue-20231231.htm), [FY2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/73309/000095017025028427/nue-20241231.htm) and [FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/73309/000119312526071575/nue-20251231.htm). Steel Dynamics' evidence comes from [FY2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1022671/000155837024002152/stld-20231231x10k.htm), [FY2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1022671/000155837025001886/stld-20241231x10k.htm) and [FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1022671/000110465926021395/stld-20251231x10k.htm). The JSON holds short literal excerpts, section locators, source URLs, availability evidence and raw-file hashes where available.

The strongest independent relevance check is contemporaneous: Steel Dynamics itself discussed increased competition from coated imports in its [17 July 2024 earnings release](https://ir.steeldynamics.com/steel-dynamics-reports-second-quarter-2024-results/). That supports investigating the product family. It does not show a particular country, exporter or later remedy is financially material.

The map deliberately separates availability from the fiscal period. Each version becomes usable at midnight New York time on the calendar day after the filing date. This conservative policy avoids pretending that an acceptance timestamp is a measured public dissemination timestamp. It does not establish that the filing contained new information. Earlier issuer disclosures may already contain the facts. For the first two Nucor versions, SEC directory dates corroborate the day; exact acceptance times were not verified.

The newest annual snapshot replaces the earlier one for experiment selection, without claiming a business began or ceased on that date. No exposure is supplied before February/March 2024. Older regulatory episodes must abstain until matched older filings are researched. Intervening quarterly/current reports and company releases must still be checked for each episode, and a 370-day staleness limit is a research guard, not a guarantee of accuracy.

This makes CORE attractive as a cheap test of Jev's ability to combine many scope, timing, product and offset questions. It remains a weak automatic stock signal: the issuer filings do not give country-specific competitive volumes, the share of products meeting the exact legal scope, contract pass-through, or incremental earnings sensitivity. Those missing quantities remain null. A large duty rate should never substitute for those missing denominators.

The next useful test is whether parallel examination of current and prior regulatory passages plus the correctly dated issuer version identifies changes that a fixed keyword packet misses. Preserve separate judgments for legal effect, product overlap, foreign/export exposure, downstream costs, magnitude and abstention. Evaluate the resulting evidence against reviewed labels before converting it into a trade direction. The issuer map is a prerequisite, not alpha evidence.

No extra listed downstream or second-order issuer was added in this bounded pass: a defensible record would need dated purchase exposure, exact product overlap and pass-through evidence. This is an evidence gap, not proof that no such issuer exists.

Research hygiene: no event return series or event-window performance was requested or used. General search results and filing repurchase tables incidentally displayed price information; therefore this should not be represented as a fully blinded human study. The map selection and facts are based on business disclosures. The four FY2023/FY2024 raw SEC documents are archived in ignored `data/exposure-sources`; FY2025 primary web text was reviewed, but raw-file capture remains pending.
