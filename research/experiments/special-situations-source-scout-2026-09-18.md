# Small-account special situations: primary-source scout

Research date: 2026-09-18. Scope: public documents, no orders, no paid calls, no return or price-outcome scan. This is a source and economic-mechanism scout, not a complete offer census or investment recommendation.

**Judgment:** cash tenders with genuine odd-lot priority deserve one bounded feasibility test. They contain a real small-account preference, but the examples below do not establish a profitable spread or a Jev-specific advantage. Do not make this the flagship project before measuring both opportunity frequency and incremental decision quality. Reverse-split rounding is particularly easy to misread.

## Three concrete document cases

**1. Arbutus (ABUS): current document-based candidate, executable economics unknown.** The issuer's August 24 announcement offers up to $230 million at $5.00–$5.75 per share, expiring September 29, 2026 at 5 p.m. New York time unless changed. [Issuer commencement announcement](https://investor.arbutusbio.com/news-releases/news-release-details/arbutus-commences-us230-million-modified-dutch-auction-tender).

The original offer gives priority to holders beneficially owning fewer than 100 shares who tender all of them at or below the final purchase price. Holdings across accounts aggregate; splitting accounts does not create eligibility. The odd-lot certification matters. Proportionate tenders are a different mechanism. There is no financing or minimum-tender condition, but termination conditions include a greater-than-10% stock-price decline and specified index declines. Canadian withholding is not automatically 25% of proceeds: estimated paid-up capital is at least C$8.42, potentially eliminating a deemed dividend; final treatment is uncertain. A broker may set an earlier deadline. [Original offer, sections 3, 5, 7 and tax discussion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1447028/000110465926100002/tm2622329d1_ex99-a1i.htm).

Arithmetic: 99 accepted shares produce $495–$569.25 gross. These are proceeds, not entry cost or profit. At 99 shares, a hypothetical $0.10 spread produces only $9.90; $0.25 produces $24.75. A bid above the clearing price can fail acceptance; bidding at the floor can accept an unprofitable price. No current executable ask, Schwab instruction cutoff, complete amendment census, or account-specific tax processing was verified here. This is not a buy signal.

**2. Wix (WIX): historical adversarial case, not a retrospective winning trade.** The March 5, 2026 commencement release specifies $1.75 billion at $80–$92 and reports a March 4 closing price of $83.78. [Issuer announcement](https://www.wix.com/press-room/home/post/wix-announces-commencement-of-modified-dutch-auctiontender-offer-to-purchase-up-to-1-750-000-000-in).

Original expiry was one minute after 11:59 p.m. New York time April 1, 2026. Eligible odd lots receive priority only when tendered at or below the final price. Electing the determined price is treated as bidding $80; the offer explicitly warns this can be below the market reference. Certified non-Israeli tax residents receive no Israeli withholding under Option 1. [Original offer, sections 1 and 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1576789/000117891326000759/exhibit_99a-1a.htm).

Arithmetic using that stale preannouncement reference solely to illustrate sizing: $1,000 affords 11 whole shares ($921.58), not 99. Their gross tender proceeds span $880–$1,012. The lower bound is a $41.58 loss before other costs. This says nothing about a tradable postannouncement entry. Later result pages appeared in search; this named example must therefore remain an extraction challenge, excluded from performance validation.

**3. Bryn (BRRN): reject the apparent micro-cash-out.** Its September 15 8-K schedules a 1-for-100,000 reverse split for September 23, 2026 at 6 p.m. PDT, with split-adjusted OTCID trading September 24. Record holders below 100,000 shares receive $0.00007 per old share; holders above that threshold have fractional new shares rounded upward. Those are distinct branches, and record-holder wording does not establish street-name beneficial treatment. [Original 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1397795/000121390026100354/ea0305670-8k_bryn.htm).

Arithmetic: the maximum small-holder cash proceeds are 99,999 × $0.00007 = $6.99993. Schwab's standard $6.95 online OTC entry commission leaves under $0.05 even before paying for the shares. This cash-out branch is uneconomic under standard pricing. Do not infer a universal one-share round-up or broker eligibility from the headline.

## Access and realistic account economics

The original documents above were readable through web extraction. Automated raw SEC access has previously been unreliable in this environment; this scout does not claim a production collection path. Archive document identity, complete text, original acceptance time, actual capture time, and every amendment before labeling a packet complete. Public search snippets cannot establish current offer status or historical availability. Missing source coverage must count as failure, not silently disappear.

Schwab's April 2026 guide lists $0 online U.S. exchange-listed stock commissions, $0 voluntary/mandatory reorganization fees, and $6.95 online OTC commissions. Broker-assisted trades are priced separately. The old blanket $39 tender-fee assumption is unsupported by the current guide. Actual tender handling, earlier deadlines, and tax certificates still need security-specific confirmation before execution. [Official pricing guide](https://www.schwab.com/legal/schwab-pricing-guide-for-individual-investors).

For a fresh position, model whole shares as `min(99, floor((1000 - fees - reserve) / executable_ask))`, then enforce all existing beneficial holdings and the particular offer's conditions. Higher capital cannot scale the same beneficial owner's odd-lot priority indefinitely. Keep economic taxes, withholding/cash lockup, spreads, termination exposure, and missed instructions separate. A conditional payout floor is not positive expected value while completion risk remains unmeasured.

## One decisive test

Pre-register a 90-day census of initial SC TO-I and SC TO-T filings and their amendments, independent of odd-lot keywords and later prices. Restrict tradability only after collecting the census: U.S.-listed cash-equity offers accessible to this account. Retain private/NAV repurchases and noncash tenders as explicit rejected records. Use historical filings only for blinded extraction and frequency measurement. Then run a 30-day prospective paper test of all newly complete offer packets and material amendments. Do not deploy a general reorganization engine yet.

Give Jev and two controls the same complete packet: (a) deterministic filing/odd-lot/range rules; (b) a capable modern single-document extractor followed by the same calculator. Jev's hypothesis is systematic testing of every relevant clause against ownership, security, tender-price, timing, tax-document, and amendment states. Require cited evidence and an unknown state. Test interacting exceptions, not merely whether the words “odd lot” occur. Deterministic code computes eligibility and cash flows after extraction. A model's confidence is neither completion probability nor an auction-price forecast.

Freeze judgments before requesting prices. Use the verified SIP access for contemporaneous quotes with displayed size, recording a fixed 30-minute manual-response delay; quotes are paper execution assumptions, not fill guarantees. Log all candidates and rejections. Accept a paper opportunity only when the lowest permitted accepted payout exceeds the ask-based cost by at least $20 after known fees and conservative unresolved cash deductions, with enough displayed size and an achievable broker deadline. Unresolved essential eligibility or tax information means abstain. Report cancellation/downside branches separately; do not label the conditional spread guaranteed profit.

**Stop rule:** do not expand this into the flagship if the 30-day census produces fewer than two independent actionable paper candidates, or Jev produces no correctly adjudicated incremental candidate or financially material avoided mistake relative to the stronger control. If it passes, continue prospectively through actual offer completion before claiming alpha. Two cases establish research yield, not profitability statistics. Reconcile the $1,000 capital schedule so overlapping opportunities cannot all spend the same cash. Report research expense separately and subtract it when evaluating whether the project actually makes money.

The strongest concern is structural: offers are explicit, infrequent documents, and a normal modern extractor may already handle them adequately. Jev becomes relevant only if its broad state testing demonstrably finds profitable exceptions or prevents consequential misclassification that the controls miss. Neither more questions nor lower inference cost would satisfy that condition.
