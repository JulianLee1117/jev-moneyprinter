# Next-family feasibility decision — 18 September 2026

**Decision: retain CORE as the extraction/rejection benchmark, and register one bounded Tenaris OCTG experiment focused on company-specific cash deposits, refunds and scope changes. Tenaris has a stronger documented link to cash flow than the current CORE-to-NUE/STLD map. It has not demonstrated stronger tradable alpha, sufficient frequency, or an informational advantage.** This is a source-based extension of the fallback already named in `PLAN.md`, not a return-selected pivot.

The current CORE audit adjudicates eight recent notices from the twenty-document sample. It establishes difficult state/timing distinctions but no fully verified economic surprise. The initial machine audit still marks all twenty dossiers unresolved; that file is not a twenty-event negative profitability result. The more recent findings are in [the economic/timing review](core-economic-timing-audit-2026-09-18.md).

## Why the fallback deserves a small test

Tenaris imports OCTG from its own foreign plants to supplement U.S. production. Its filings connect the Argentina and Mexico proceedings directly to paid deposits, operating costs and potential refunds. That is a shorter causal chain than estimating how restrictions on someone else's supply affect a diversified domestic competitor. It is still mixed: domestic production can benefit from protection while the company's imports bear duties. [Tenaris FY2025 20-F, risk factors and Note 27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1190723/000155485526000490/ts-20251231.htm)

The company's February 2026 results release identifies a **$34 million FY2025 gain** related to Argentine OCTG antidumping deposits, against **$2.899 billion annual EBITDA**: about **1.17%**, calculated as 34/2,899. This verifies a real financial channel and also warns against equating a large rate change with a huge company-level effect. This later financial outcome is family-feasibility evidence only; it cannot be supplied as an input to a purported June 2025 prediction or used to label June 2025 surprise. [Tenaris 18 February 2026 results](https://ir.tenaris.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tenaris-announces-2025-fourth-quarter-and-annual-results)

Three distinctions make this suitable for Jev's parallel interpretation:

- **Proposal versus operative rate:** Argentina's December 2024 proposal was **6.8%**. The June 2025 final was **6.76%**, while the previous operative deposit rate was **78.30%**. The enormous operative reduction was largely signaled by the proposal; only a **0.04 percentage-point** final-versus-proposal reduction occurred. Lower deposits, retrospective assessment, litigation and receipt of cash have separate clocks. [Preliminary notice](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-12-09/html/2024-28859.htm), [final notice](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-06-12/html/2025-10654.htm), [Tenaris explanation](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1190723/000155485526000490/ts-20251231.htm)
- **Pending company-specific exposure:** Mexico's May 2026 preliminary review gives TAMSA **1.62%**, compared with **26.10%** in the prior final review. The arithmetic difference is **24.48 percentage points**; the new preliminary notice makes prospective deposits contingent on publication of the final results. It does not already implement 1.62%. Any follow-up needs the actual intervening orders, memoranda and CBP instructions. [May 2026 preliminary](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-05-13/html/2026-09464.htm), [September 2025 final](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-09-05/html/2025-17071.htm)
- **Scope and cash uncertainty:** Tenaris disclosed a CBP audit calculation of roughly **$49.6 million** for mechanical/other pipe and disputed whether those products fall within OCTG scope; it reported no provision for those historical imports because the loss was not considered probable or reliably estimable. This is a contingent claim, not established company liability or immediate profit. A ruling that resolves scope might be more useful than another broad industry vote, but public availability and the current legal posture must be verified. [Tenaris Q1 2026 statements, Note 27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1190723/000117184326003164/f6k_050626fs.htm)

There is an additional source conflict to preserve: the 2025 Mexico Federal Register notice says deposit requirements apply from publication, September 5, whereas Tenaris describes September 15 as its deposit-rate change date. This review does not resolve that discrepancy. Retrieve operative CBP instructions and any amendments before choosing an effective date. [Official notice](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-09-05/html/2025-17071.htm), [company filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1190723/000155485526000490/ts-20251231.htm)

## Bounded source census

A fresh Federal Register API query for the exact phrase `oil country tubular goods`, dated **2024-01-01 through 2026-09-18**, returned **116 records**, all on one complete response page. **57** contain the phrase in their titles. Only **six** also name Argentina or Mexico in the title: `2024-11726`, `2024-28476`, `2024-28859`, `2025-10654`, `2025-17071`, and `2026-09464`.

The response is saved in [the raw census](../data/octg-fr-discovery-2026-09-18.json), with its query, content hash and exact selected records in [the census manifest](octg-feasibility-census.v1.json). These are document counts, not opportunities. Omnibus review notices, court decisions, scope memoranda and CBP material may add relevant records; a title screen is incomplete. Country and review stages are dependent. The six titles provide no basis for claiming a large independent sample or scheduling a final ruling from a nominal deadline.

This sparse census is a reason to limit spending. The current evidence favors stronger **attribution**, not stronger **frequency** or **predictability**. The heavily publicized historical reductions are development cases, and the issuer disclosures are evidence that the information is already visible to investors.

## Exact next experiment

Register `OCTG-TS-cashflow-v1`, separate from CORE. Freeze the six census documents as a source-discovery seed, their relevant prior operative orders, and issuer disclosures available before each candidate decision. Argentina A-357-824 and Mexico A-201-856 stay separate, with common parent-case dependence recorded. Do not add countries merely to increase the count. No price work or paid inference was performed for this fallback check.

Construct one current-state packet per actual legal increment, then use Jev to screen each passage across parallel questions for exporter identity, exact product scope, proposal/operative status, assessment versus forward deposit, court or liquidation block, issuer importer role, economic direction by channel, and evidence gaps. Code computes rate differences and cash-flow bounds only when the denominator is supported. No multiplication of question probabilities into a trading probability.

The economic worksheet should distinguish: expected cash release from old entries, recurring changes on new import value, potential domestic-production pricing effects, taxes, timing and legal uncertainty. Unknown imported value or accumulated deposit amount remains unknown. A directionally favorable duty change cannot be labeled a material equity catalyst solely from its percentage rate.

Compare this broad Jev screen with deterministic rate/case matching and a cheap conventional model given the same corpus and a matched total inference/review budget. Score missed operative changes, false alerts, company-link correctness and review effort against independently reviewed evidence. First solve source timing, exposure and financial magnitude for several distinct episodes; then freeze a small prospective paper protocol or a separately labeled historical diagnostic. No new family should earn trading capital from extraction performance.

**Stop condition:** if the bounded evidence cannot yield multiple independently timed, plausibly material company cash-flow increments, retain it as a useful legal extraction fixture and move on with the failed feasibility result recorded. Do not expand it into a general trading platform to compensate for a thin opportunity set. At present neither CORE nor OCTG is a demonstrated money-making strategy.
