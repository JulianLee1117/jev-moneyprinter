Reviewed September 18, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. This independent agent review used saved requests, responses, the final frozen signal file, and the attempt ledger. It made no paid calls or network requests and estimated no returns. The reproducible counts, per-document distributions, costs, input fields and source hashes are in [the accompanying JSON](core-daily-signal-review-2026-09-18.json).

**The frozen Jev rule produced no active observations. This is evidence that this particular input contract and decision rule were unproductive, not that Jev found no financial alpha.** Every input lacked a separately supplied prior case document, while the directional options required explicit comparable prior treatment. That mismatch merits investigation. It is not proven to be the sole cause: current notices can themselves describe old treatment, and no matched prior-state ablation was performed.

## What the models returned

Jev completed all twenty documents. Its competitive-channel choices were thirteen `none`, three `negative`, one `mixed`, and three `positive`; it never selected `unknown`. The three positive cases were 2016-20429 at 0.47, 2025-21761 at 0.39, and 2025-23430 at 0.61. All were below the frozen 0.70 option-probability threshold. Therefore the mechanical explanation for all-cash is seventeen nonpositive choices plus three subthreshold positives—not twenty declarations of insufficient knowledge. Three negative choices also cannot become active observations under the registered long-only rule.

Jev's other distributions were:

- Current change: eight operative changes, four proposals, eight procedure/no-change cases.
- Prior comparison: one explicit, eight partial, eleven absent.
- Potential headline exception: thirteen present and seven not identified.

The conventional baseline returned nineteen validated responses and one invalid response retained as unknown. Among the valid responses it chose eight positive channels, eight none and three negative. Seven positives met 0.70; one did not. Its other distributions were ten operative changes, five proposals and four procedure/no-change cases; eight explicit, seven partial and four absent prior comparisons; and seventeen present versus two unidentified exceptions. These counts use nineteen valid answers, not a silently reduced nineteen-document cohort: the twentieth document remains unknown in the frozen evaluation.

These are distributions, not accuracy scores. No independently adjudicated answer key exists for this twenty-document exercise. A model that generates more trades is not consequently better. Conversely, abstention does not establish correctness. One useful consistency warning is the baseline's 2021-17793 answer: it labeled current status `no_change_or_procedure`, yet assigned positive competitive direction with probability 0.86. The existing Türkiye audit also shows that a direction can be wrong when exporter changes oppose one another. Neither warning should be erased by a profitable-looking later return.

## Why the contract is not a meaningful test of broad Jev discovery

All twenty requests supplied zero prior-document records with prior completeness marked unknown. All also supplied zero dated issuer exposures, zero separately verified earlier-public evidence records, and zero validated numeric rate records. Both models instead saw the same fixed keyword/neighborhood excerpt of the current notice, with a 10,000-byte retrieval budget. This was fair for equal-input comparison but did not exercise broad Jev retrieval or the source-to-issuer links proposed as its advantage.

The directional criteria require the restriction to strengthen or weaken against explicitly supplied previous treatment, with no material offset shown. Some notices contain such comparisons inline; empty structured prior state alone does not invalidate every answer. But Jev judged nineteen of twenty comparisons incomplete or absent. None of its three positive choices had explicit prior support: two were partial, one absent. That supports a *plausible input limitation*, not a causal proof that filling the field will unlock useful trades.

The uncalibrated 0.70 threshold also treats native Jev distributions and conventional self-reported probabilities as if their numerical scales were interchangeable. This is acceptable as a transparently fixed development rule, but not evidence that one model is intrinsically more confident or financially predictive. Lowering it after seeing all-cash would create a new development experiment; it must not retroactively improve this one.

## Next investigation

Preserve the entire frozen run and close its signal-generation result as **zero active Jev observations, incomplete conventional comparison, and unavailable prices**. Do not lower the threshold or selectively reinterpret cases to manufacture trades. The price block can be resolved separately with a compatible lawful data source; this all-cash Jev arm would still contain no active observations.

The most useful model experiment now supplies the information the question actually needs: source-bound prior state, identifiable issuer claims, current amounts, and later changes. For direct company cash claims, Jev can screen entire filings for outstanding versus realized amounts, customer pass-through or other cash offsets, and subsequent receipts or settlement. That is a concrete use of inexpensive parallel judgments over a broad source instead of asking one incomplete excerpt to infer an economic delta.

Freeze a new corpus and question contract before its outputs, retain every source span, and compare independently reviewed support against ordinary retrieval. Separate claims that are still outstanding from amounts already received; a historical refund mentioned in an annual report is not a fresh catalyst. Proceed to the money question only with an explicit mechanism and timing, while allowing a separate conservative daily association diagnostic. The next dataset should determine whether discovery is useful, rather than rewarding another round of prompt tuning on known CORE fixtures.
