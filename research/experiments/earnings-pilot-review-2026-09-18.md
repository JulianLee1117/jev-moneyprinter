# Earnings return pilot: pipeline completed, no alpha demonstrated

The first earnings experiment reached actual historical prices. The frozen positive-mean semantic rule lost money in the account illustration. Native Jev probabilities performed worse than categorical Jev and produced the same selected trades as the conventional model. Do not deploy, reverse, or tune this rule using these outcomes.

## What was fixed before prices

A deterministic twelve-issuer development cohort was selected from the previously captured official 2026 Q2 filing index. Eight original earnings-release pairs were accessible; four source failures remained in the cohort. Direct SEC exhibit downloads were denied, so paired issuer releases were captured as current versions, without proof that their bytes match the original publication. This is not a representative historical small-cap universe: current-listing survival and size limitations remain.

Each model received the same deterministic selection of original passages from each prior/current release, with omissions recorded. Jev answered eight business-change questions in parallel. Its score was the mean of favourable minus adverse native probability mass; categorical Jev and GPT-5.4-mini used corresponding categorical scores. All sixteen calls were valid. Every output and failure was frozen before event-price capture. No models, thresholds, holding periods or passages changed after observing returns.

Entry was 09:35 ET on the second full session after the SEC acceptance date. Exit was 09:35 five sessions later. Pre-entry eligibility required a last close of at least $5 and twenty-session mean dollar volume of at least $1 million. RGCO and MVST failed those rules; ten events remained eligible, six with complete model outputs.

## Measured results

All twelve events have adjusted daily return proxies. Eleven have stock/IWM quotes for the frozen windows; RGCO's missing exit quote remains missing. All six eligible, model-complete cases had negative stock returns and negative IWM-relative returns. Their mean five-session adjusted-close excess return was **−3.58 percentage points**. Their separate whole-share quote-account excess return averaged **−5.73 percentage points**. Neither average is a portfolio return.

The $1,000 account illustration used one position at a time, whole shares, cash available only after settlement, first regular two-sided quotes, displayed share-size checks, 10 basis points adverse slippage per side, and a $1 round-trip fee allowance. Inference fees were charged once per arm.

- **Jev native distributions:** approximately **−$94.92**, ending near $905.08.
- **Jev categories:** approximately **−$79.79**, ending near $920.21.
- **Conventional model:** approximately **−$94.95**, ending near $905.05.
- **Always-long, deterministic event-ID priority:** approximately **−$79.79**.
- **Cash:** $0 gain/loss.

Every arm first selected BYD. For the next available entry, native Jev and the conventional model selected IFF; categorical Jev and always-long selected AGCO. Thus native probability ranking worsened price-only P&L by **$15.13** versus categorical Jev and added zero versus the conventional model before inference fees. All ten eligible events were retained: the four missing-source events occurred while capital was occupied, so their missing signals were not converted into cash signals or silently dropped.

At 25 basis points per side, native Jev lost approximately $100.32 and categorical Jev approximately $85.07. Total actual inference charges for both model arms were $0.036794496; cost savings are not evidence of alpha.

## Interpretation and limits

These are current-history, quote-based illustrations, not fills or verified executable historical profits. Corporate-action history, issuer distribution dates and stable adjusted/raw price ratios corroborated no in-window adjustment for the selected BYD, AGCO, IFF and corresponding IWM windows. The initial conservative action-unresolved ledger is preserved separately. Corroboration does not establish complete historical source availability or eliminate provider omissions.

The sample has only two sequential account trades and six eligible complete-source events clustered in one earnings season. The mean native unknown mass is substantial; a positive score is not a high-confidence profit forecast. There is no fitted return model, numerical earnings-surprise baseline, statistical significance claim or untouched validation sample. Historical model memory, incomplete retrieval, issuer-version uncertainty, prior-release comparability and intervening guidance remain limitations. The result neither establishes alpha nor statistically rejects the broader use of semantic features.

## Next decision

Keep the return pipeline. Retire the interpretation that a positive average business-language score is itself a trade. Before further strategy claims, assemble a much broader, dated cohort with numerical news and pre-entry price controls; compare equally constrained predictive models using those controls alone, compact-model categories, Jev categories and full Jev distributions. Keep this pilot in development, require chronological separation and prospective confirmation, and do not select a new threshold or invert the strategy because these six cases fell. No live orders are justified by this result.
