# Semiconductor read-through: bounded source scout, 2026-09-18

The best next family is semiconductor capital-spending composition and timing, mapped to AMAT, LRCX and KLAC. Start with all scheduled TSMC and Micron results, not selected dramatic cuts. This has a direct customer-to-equipment spending channel, repeated official disclosures and important distinctions that can reverse a headline interpretation. It is a testable hypothesis, not evidence of profitable alpha.

The companion [development fixtures](read-through-development-fixtures.v1.json) contain three price-blind examples, 18 atomic questions, source dates and URLs, and separately stored reviewer expectations. The model must receive only the allowlisted evidence fields and question instructions. Exclude expected answers, rationales and economic mappings. These are handpicked method fixtures, not independent alpha observations or a profitability sample. No prices, paid inference or trading were used in this scout.

The fixtures cover:

- TSMC July–October 2022: a same-year spending revision with both equipment timing and capacity adjustments. The prior source already disclosed some deferral. The source does not allocate a lost revenue figure to any supplier.
- Micron June–September 2023: the total-budget and equipment directions differ, with construction and process intensity affecting interpretation. The previously announced equipment direction must be separated from a new surprise.
- Intel April–July 2022: the GAAP and net-spending columns require separate treatment. Cash offsets prevent a direct conversion of a net-budget revision into tool demand.

Exact short excerpts are in the public v1 JSON; the report deliberately does not repeat them. That compact version includes reviewer paraphrases and must not be used to claim raw-source extraction accuracy. The separate internal `data/readthrough-development-v1/source-fixtures.v2.json` supplies exact archived source spans, no paraphrases, 18 questions and four independently reviewed interpretation routes. Expected answers and routes remain outside model inputs.

## What would make this a Jev experiment

Use one shared packet containing the new disclosure, the last comparable guidance for that fiscal period, intervening updates, and supplier exposure cards that existed before the event. Ask independent questions for each supplier and channel: buildings versus tools, timing versus cancellation, capacity versus process intensity, financing offsets, exposure support, novelty and missing counterevidence. Preserve uncertainty. Do not multiply separate probabilities or interpret them as probabilities of a profitable trade.

Jev could make broad, repeated interpretation practical, but cheaper parallel classification is not financial alpha. An efficient contemporary chat model can perform the same economic reasoning. Success requires a better decision or useful coverage at the decision deadline, followed by superior net outcomes. Do not increase question counts to manufacture a native-model advantage.

Use three substantive controls: a deterministic same-period numeric budget-revision rule; an explicit WFE-direction rule that also recognizes repeated guidance, construction and net/gross comparability; and compact categorical chat on identical evidence. Add cash and an always-long same-universe benchmark. A deliberately naive total-capex rule is an explanatory diagnostic, not the only serious rival.

## Reach a return measurement quickly

1. Freeze the calendar census of every TSMC/Micron quarterly announcement from 2025-01-01 through 2026-09-18, including no-change, missing and incomparable cases. This is roughly fourteen buyer-event clusters before audit, not hundreds of independent trades. AMAT/LRCX/KLAC responses to one release are correlated observations. Do not count questions or suppliers as new event samples.
2. Assemble and hash each source packet, its first-seen evidence and earlier comparable guidance before price retrieval. An absent prior total forecast remains absent. Management's revision is distinct from a surprise relative to market consensus. Do not substitute year-over-year spending growth for either.
3. Freeze all model decisions and deterministic controls, then obtain actual historical quotes. The proposed second-US-session 09:35 entry is a defensible slow-reaction diagnostic only when the packet was available by that time. A fixed two-day delay does not prove availability of a subsequently corrected transcript. In unverifiable cases use a release-only packet with established availability, or mark the row unavailable. A late entry also deliberately gives up the immediate move; that is the strategy being tested.
4. Pick one primary horizon before prices: five US sessions. One session can be a declared secondary result. Measure entry ask to exit bid, regular-session valid quotes, stock splits and costs. Apply the same timing and executable quote rules to every arm. SOXX-relative returns are a diagnostic comparison, not a claim of an executed hedge.
5. Report both normalized event returns and a cash-constrained $1,000 simulation with feasible whole-share orders, overlapping positions, cash drag and no borrowed shares. Do not spend the same capital independently on every event. Keep API/data research expense visible separately from trading spread and slippage.
6. This small historical run answers whether the pipeline can make measurable, economically distinct decisions. Contemporary models may know old events: it cannot be called a genuine holdout. Freeze the next prospective specification before new announcements, preserve every episode, and evaluate completed outcomes without revising rules after seeing returns. A 60-day continuation with two quarterly reporters cannot establish alpha by itself.

If the source method works, extend the same frozen rule to the complete 2018–2025 census of TSMC, Micron, Intel, Samsung and SK hynix: at most about 160 scheduled buyer-quarter events before missing data and overlapping clusters. Expansion must be calendar driven, not conditioned on profitable-looking cases. Current model hindsight still limits historical evidence. Do not build the large platform before measuring the first executable return study.

## Evidence, exposures and practical stop rules

There is a published basis for related-company underreaction, but it is neither new nor proof of current profitability. Cohen and Frazzini's historical customer-link study uses an old monthly-return sample. Ali and Hirshleifer show that shared analyst links capture much of several return-spillover patterns; common information and sector momentum therefore need serious controls. See [Economic Links and Predictable Returns](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~afrazzin/pdf/Economic%20Links%20and%20Predictable%20Returns%20-%20Cohen%20and%20Frazzini.pdf) and [Shared Analyst Coverage: Unifying Momentum Spillover Effects](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25201).

Supplier concentration can anchor the channel but must be measured as of each event. For example, [Applied Materials' 2024 report](https://investors.appliedmaterials.com/static-files/ea7dd925-860f-4b1c-8121-90655958b3a7) identifies significant TSMC and Samsung concentration. [Lam's 2024 report](https://filecache.investorroom.com/mr5ir_lamresearch2/1414/LAM%20RESEARCH%20CORP_BOOKMARKS_2024_V1.pdf) identifies major customers and discloses memory/foundry/logic equipment mix; that mix is not a share of total company revenue. [KLA's 2024 filing](https://ir.kla.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000319201-24-000021/klac-20240630.htm) identifies large-customer concentration. These later filings cannot be injected into 2022–2023 decisions. The fixtures deliberately contain no asserted point-in-time supplier weighting.

Alpaca's [market-data FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq) says historical SIP requests with an end time at least 15 minutes old do not require a real-time subscription. Verify account entitlement and the actual response feed before relying on this. Its [historical quote endpoint](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockquotesingle-1) supports bounded quote requests and pagination. IEX-only observations are not an NBBO substitute; quote availability is not proof of a fill.

Stop or revise the method before scaling if it makes unsupported procurement/revenue inferences in these explicit counterexamples, or cannot preserve missing prior state. Stop the financial hypothesis after the frozen cohort if no decisions differ from strong simple controls, or apparent advantages disappear under observed spreads and prespecified slippage. A few positive clustered returns warrant a prospective test, not an alpha claim. If the whole historical study has very few distinct positive decisions, label it underpowered; adding more questions will not create more evidence.
