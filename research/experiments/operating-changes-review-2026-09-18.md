# Operating-change experiment: implemented, source gate rejected

Completed September 18, 2026 Pacific time (artifacts use September 19 UTC timestamps).

The frozen source sample does **not** support advancing this family to a return study. Independent agent review covered all 749 source records and all 779 record/vendor pairs. No episode satisfied every registered requirement: attributable customer, paid production use, completed or active change, business link, identifiable organization and sufficiently explicit transition timing. The threshold was twenty distinct episodes across four vendors.

These are insufficient-evidence findings under a specific rule, not proof that the reported migrations never occurred or that every SaaS information strategy fails. Reviews are agent judgments, not human gold labels. No returns or profitable trades were measured.

## What was built and exercised

The standard-library Python CLI now captures immutable source envelopes, enforces a separate Scry budget, samples product mentions without action-word or popularity filters, merges duplicates, resolves known thread links and freezes development/evaluation groups before inference. Jev evaluates twelve dimensions per record/vendor and retains its full native distributions. The same text is available to keyword retrieval, pinned semantic reranking and a compact categorical chat control.

The first SQL attempt exceeded Scry's work limit and returned no records. An archived pre-cohort correction used the source index and current-state semantics without changing vendors, quotas, dates or sample seed. All sixteen corrected source queries completed. Unfilled source/vendor quotas were preserved. Exactly 500 original Hacker News API requests resolved 263 of 278 missing native-reference roots; fifteen references remain unresolved. Known intermediate ancestry is also grouped. The frozen split contains 136 development and 613 evaluation records.

The implementation includes source-bound independent review, comparison provenance checks, preservation of unknowns, and continuation of previously unattempted calls without retrying failed requests. It also includes a read-only historical SIP price adapter. Its entitlement smoke receipt says `missing_credentials`; no market prices were downloaded.

## Observed screening results

Jev attempted 394 requests containing 9,348 individual judgments. Of these, 390 responses and 9,264 judgments passed the frozen validation rules. Four responses remain invalid/unknown with their original bytes and costs retained. One example selected `unknown` with probability 0.49 while assigning 0.50 to `unchanged`; the registered choice/distribution consistency rule rejected that response. Nothing was renormalized or relabeled to improve the result.

Jev selected 28 candidate record/vendor pairs; keywords selected 66 and semantic retrieval selected 162. All were independently reviewed and none met every economic qualification. Broad screening candidates are not verified episodes, and this finding does not measure the accuracy of every individual model attribute.

The chat control completed 44 valid development requests. Six attempted requests failed, with the last two explicitly reporting HTTP 429. A deliberate continuation reused cached successes, preserved failed requests and sent only previously unattempted requests. Further paid calls were stopped after the full source audit rejected the family. The conventional evaluation arm was not submitted. Consequently, the complete four-arm comparison is **incomplete**, even though the complete source-density assessment says **stop this family**. Missing outcomes are not counted as negatives.

On the 43 development requests with valid responses from both models, reported Jev API fees were **$0.009035754**, versus **$0.16988625** for compact chat: approximately **18.8 times lower**. This comparison excludes failed calls, source acquisition, agent review, engineering work and unavailable requests. It demonstrates lower API fees on a matched subset, not accuracy superiority, lower total operating cost or trading alpha. Raw unknown retention creates substantial review work; total review-cost savings have not been established. Recall cannot be estimated against a reviewed sample with zero qualifying positives.

## Spending and decision

The ledgers report **$0.257176638** in completed/invalid-response model costs and zero reported Scry charges. Uncertain attempts retain **$0.252181751** in conservative reservations. Total accounted exposure is **$0.509358389**, within the $15 experiment cap. Reservations are not confirmed charges. There were no failed-request retries or orders.

Keep the implementation and archived failures. Do not buy prices or allocate trading capital to this sample. A next source pilot should establish dated, attributable operating changes and vendor-relevant economic magnitude from original engineering or similarly direct reports before funding another financial test. Scry production usage remains unresolved under its [pricing](https://scry.io/#pricing) and [terms](https://scry.io/legal/terms); this run was registered as private technical evaluation.

Validation: **342 tests passed**, including budgets, credential handling, source hashing, copied/thread-connected records, partial ancestry, complete text coverage, pinned rerank validation, resumable calls, comparison provenance, missing controls and incomplete reviews.
