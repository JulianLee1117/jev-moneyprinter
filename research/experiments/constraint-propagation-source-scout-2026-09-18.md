# Constraint propagation source scout — 2026-09-18

Choose **industrial nitrogen production and distribution constraints** over FDA facility enforcement for the next bounded source-feasibility gate. This is a research preference, not evidence of profitable trading. The strongest candidate is a dated change in an outage's scope or expected duration, propagated through product compatibility, competing capacity, contracts and costs to an issuer's prospective cash contribution. US-listed CF, NTR and LXU provide an accessible initial equity universe; no current quotes, execution eligibility, spreads or position sizes were checked.

This scout inspected primary issuer/regulator documents, using targeted full sections rather than a complete event census. It made no paid calls and examined no equity return series. The cases below are selected mechanism examples, not an unbiased historical test. All source bodies were observed now; displayed document dates do not independently establish the historical availability of these exact versions.

## Three source cases

### 1. Nutrien Trinidad: real removed supply, but not a simple short of the owner

[Nutrien's October 21, 2025 announcement](https://www.nutrien.com/news/press-releases/nutrien-commences-a-controlled-shut-down-of-its-trinidad-nitrogen-operations-1735) displays 5:01 a.m., without a timezone in the rendered date. It announced a controlled shutdown effective October 23 of its Point Lisas nitrogen operations, citing port restrictions and unreliable/uneconomic gas. Disclosed sales were approximately **85,000 metric tonnes of ammonia and 55,000 tonnes of urea per month**. The company nevertheless maintained annual nitrogen volume guidance, citing North American performance. Thus gross local disruption is not the owner's incremental consolidated earnings loss.

The stated run rate corresponds arithmetically to 255,000 tonnes of ammonia and 165,000 tonnes of urea over three months. This is a pre-offset sales-volume scenario, not verified lost production, accessible competitor orders or profit. Do not add ammonia feedstock a second time if it is embedded in upgraded urea, or interchange metric tonnes with US short tons.

[National Energy's October 23, 2025 statement](https://nationalenergy.tt/Portals/0/Documents/Approved%20Media%20Release%20-%20National%20Energy%20Reaffirms%20Commitment%20to%20the%20Energy%20Sector.pdf) gives the counterparty's account of port-rate negotiations. The two accounts provide useful competing causal claims; neither alone establishes how quickly shipping access could resume.

[Nutrien's 2025 results](https://www.nutrien.com/news/press-releases/nutrien-reports-full-year-2025-results-and-provides-2026-guidance-1741) subsequently said Trinidad and New Madrid together supplied about 1.6 million tonnes in 2025 but contributed marginal free cash flow. That is hindsight validation of the need for a margin filter, not information available on the original announcement. [August 5, 2026 results](https://www.nutrien.com/news/press-releases/nutrien-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-1753) still reported no Trinidad production and associated safe-mode costs.

**Potential cash channel:** available low-cost rival ammonia/urea production may obtain more volume or higher realized netbacks. Missing: incremental rival sales allocation, spare capacity, shipping reach, inventory buffers, contract repricing and attribution against other global supply changes. This case supports an economic model, not a proved beneficiary or entry.

### 2. CF Yazoo City: scope and restart revisions matter; apparent substitutes need product-level checks

[CF's 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324404/000132440426000007/cf-20251231.htm) identifies an incident in the AN upgrade area in November 2025. Other units were undamaged but the entire site was idled; the then-expected restart was fourth-quarter 2026 at the earliest. Facility product mix and storage are disclosed. This is much more specific than an undifferentiated headline about a fertilizer plant.

The [August 5, 2026 issuer update](https://ir.cfindustries.com/Investors/news/news-details/2026/CF-Industries-Holdings-Inc--Reports-First-Half-2026-Net-Earnings-of-1-34-Billion-Adjusted-EBITDA-of-2-18-Billion/default.aspx) moved expected restart to **first-half 2027**, including ammonia, AN solution, nitric acid, UAN and urea liquor. It reported 98% utilization of available ammonia capacity, with Yazoo excluded from that denominator. Consequently, treating the remaining 2% as spare capacity relative to the entire historical network would be wrong. It also reported insurance expectations and substantial non-operating settlement income, which must be separated from production economics. The [June 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324404/000132440426000019/cf-20260630.htm) describes property-damage/business-interruption insurance claims and the revised rebuild decision. An outage's cash loss is not simply lost tonnes times gross margin when insurance may reimburse it.

**Candidate rival LXU is economically relevant, but not interchangeable.** [LSB's October 30, 2025 10-Q, page 22](https://investors.lsbindustries.com/static-files/c6f3fcbc-2bc8-4443-976b-db74096bae89) says it ended fertilizer-grade high-density AN production in Q3 2025 and shifted toward industrial/mining AN solution under contracts with feedstock pass-through. This source precedes the November 5 Yazoo incident; its SEC accession is [0001193125-25-258545](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/60714/000119312525258545/lxu-20250930.htm). It also explains that presales can separate shipment-period realized prices from spot prices. That rules out assigning every lost CF fertilizer ton to LSB at spot margins; it does not rule out AN-solution competition. The Q3 filing is **after** the October 21 Trinidad notice, so its newly disclosed information must not be used at that earlier cutoff.

[LSB's February 25, 2026 outlook](https://investors.lsbindustries.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lsb-industries-inc-reports-operating-results-2025-fourth-quarter) disclosed its own maintenance constraint: roughly 60,000 tons of ammonia and 50,000 tons of UAN production impact in 2026. The [July 29, 2026 results](https://investors.lsbindustries.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lsb-industries-inc-reports-operating-results-2026-second-quarter) explicitly link producer outages to favorable industrial spot/contract pricing. AN/nitric-acid volumes rose 11%, while ammonia volumes fell 46%; adjusted EBITDA was $53.1 million, but $28.8 million of turnaround costs was excluded. These reported outcomes validate both a supply/price mechanism and major offsets, without attributing any result solely to Yazoo or demonstrating that an earlier stock trade would have earned alpha.

**Most promising unresolved question:** can a newly disclosed restart delay imply a material next-quarter change in *uncommitted, compatible, deliverable* rival production before that change is reflected in issuer guidance? A long-only $1,000 account needs a positive beneficiary with this evidence; identifying only the impaired owner does not create a practical short strategy.

### 3. Glenmark Indore: FDA enforcement fails the cash bridge in this scout

The [FDA warning letter dated July 11, 2025](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/glenmark-pharmaceuticals-limited-708270-07112025) identifies facility FEI 3008565058, inspected February 3–14, and refers to potassium-chloride ER quality failures. Other products are redacted. The current page is explicitly **amended**, while retaining the original letter date. Using today's full text as if it was publicly visible on July 11 would therefore be unjustified. Glenmark's [July 12 issuer notice](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/GLENMARK_12072025213500_UPDATE_BSE_NSE_12072025.pdf) also references an earlier May 9 OAI disclosure: inspection, classification, issuer notice, warning date and public posting are distinct clocks.

This evidence does not establish a complete affected-product list, incremental stopped US supply, competitor available capacity, net pricing or material US-listed beneficiary earnings. FDA's [shortage FAQ](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-shortages/frequently-asked-questions-about-drug-shortages) explains that shortages are national supply/demand assessments and mitigation can include increased production, expiry extensions and temporary imports. A warning or local unavailability alone is insufficient. **Reject this case for equity testing without additional product-level economic evidence.**

## What Jev would have to add

Build a versioned **facility → process/product → delivery market → listed owner/competitor → contract → cash contribution** graph. Give Jev compact, directly relevant source pairs and ask atomic applicability questions: same product grade, actually operating, facility ownership, relevant contract, or disclosed spare supply. Keep explicit unknowns. Evaluate many candidate edges against the same evidence, then let code propagate the constraints; do not ask Jev to perform hidden multi-hop inference or arithmetic over a long unrelated state. Counterevidence such as closure of a cash-negative asset or a competitor's own maintenance becomes additional independently grounded graph constraints.

Then a deterministic calculation layer enforces mass balance, ownership and unit conversion. An upper bound on redirected volume is the minimum of verified lost merchant supply, uncommitted compatible rival capacity and deliverable demand. Existing-volume repricing is a separate channel. Incremental contribution must subtract feedstock, freight, displaced-product opportunity cost, maintenance and other cash expenses; it must not count upstream ammonia and its downstream product twice. For unavailable quantities, retain a range including zero rather than inventing a central estimate.

Jev's distributions are evidence-assessment outputs, not calibrated probabilities of stock returns. Multiplying independent affirmative scores would not establish joint causal confidence. The proposed advantage is higher-quality constrained synthesis across documents, with fewer economically important mapping mistakes. An ordinary LLM and a deterministic graph/keyword baseline can also do this; Jev-specific incremental accuracy remains to be measured on identical evidence.

## Source and trading limits; proposed next gate

- Freeze every document version with first observation, displayed release/filing time, event time and supersession relationships. Use conservative next-session availability where a public posting time cannot be verified. The examples above are current-version feasibility examples, not valid historical signals.
- Public issuer disclosures provide capacities and quarterly realized prices, but free high-frequency wholesale prices, utilization, inventories and contract timing may be inadequate. USDA's [August 10, 2026 fertilizer-data notice](https://www.ams.usda.gov/content/usda-announces-availability-new-fertilizer-transportation-data) confirms public production, inventory, import, regional-price and transport datasets. Its [October 17, 2024 methodology discussion](https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/GTR10172024.pdf) says the price dashboard uses monthly averages derived from weekly Green Markets reports. Do not treat that as timely transaction prices or free permission to redistribute the underlying feed.
- Commodity, gas, crop-demand, weather and geopolitical shocks can dominate the chosen outage. Multi-month disruptions fit manual equity research better than an intraday power-price race, but duration does not imply information remains unpriced. No event-study returns or tradeable edge have been established here.
- NYSE listings for CF, NTR and LXU are explicit in their linked releases. Actual Schwab eligibility, current price, spread, liquidity and whole-share sizing remain execution checks. A 5% gain on $1,000 is $50 before costs/tax; research costs cannot be justified merely by finding a technically correct story. Avoid relying on options, leverage or short availability to make this pilot feasible.

**Recommendation:** permit a small source gate, not a full trading build. Freeze a census of all capacity-loss and restart-duration notices from CF, NTR and LXU, plus directly named counterparties, published 2025-09-19 through 2026-09-18. Cap it at 12 economic episodes / 40 primary documents and reject rather than fill with generic commentary. Require at least three episodes with timestampable *new* information, exact products/ownership, a practical US-listed beneficiary and a cash-contribution range whose optimistic case is material relative to that issuer's quarter. Treat 5% of quarterly operating cash contribution as a proposed preregistered screening threshold, not an empirical optimum; show sensitivity around it. If those inputs cannot be obtained publicly, stop this niche before paid model or price work. If they can, compare Jev against the same graph with an ordinary model on withheld economic-path labels, then separately test delayed, costed equity outcomes. No claim that this is the highest-profit-probability strategy is supported yet.

Relative to a periodic earnings/capex-composition study, this scout has stronger physical supply quantities but weaker demonstrated event frequency and earnings attribution. Only two economic outage episodes were investigated, and neither established a positive material beneficiary using information available before its first notice. The FDA case failed the bridge. Thus keep this as a secondary small triage; do not displace a better-populated periodic-disclosure experiment on the evidence here. A third source-qualified event and defensible cash bounds are needed before promoting it.

**Pair A — same commodity exposure, not proven beneficiary.**

- Constraint source: [Nutrien, October 21, 2025](https://www.nutrien.com/news/press-releases/nutrien-commences-a-controlled-shut-down-of-its-trinidad-nitrogen-operations-1735): “Ammonia and urea sales volumes from Nutrien’s Trinidad operations are approximately 85 and 55 thousand tonnes per month, respectively.”
- Candidate source: [LSB, October 15, 2025](https://investors.lsbindustries.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lsb-industries-inc-schedules-2025-third-quarter-results-release): “manufactures ammonia and ammonia-related products at facilities in Cherokee, Alabama, El Dorado, Arkansas and Pryor, Oklahoma”. This dated issuer description precedes the constraint announcement. For quantitative confirmation available at that cutoff, [July 29 results](https://investors.lsbindustries.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lsb-industries-inc-reports-operating-results-2025-second-quarter) report 66,069 short tons of ammonia sold in Q2; do not use the later October 29 quarter retrospectively.
- Atomic question: Does the candidate manufacture one of the named constrained commodities, specifically ammonia? Expected label: **Yes**. Spare deliverable capacity, incremental orders, repricing and profit: **Unknown**. The positive product match is not an earnings claim.

**Pair B — completed product exit defeats an exact-grade replacement claim.**

- Constraint source: [CF's August 5, 2026 update](https://ir.cfindustries.com/Investors/news/news-details/2026/CF-Industries-Holdings-Inc--Reports-First-Half-2026-Net-Earnings-of-1-34-Billion-Adjusted-EBITDA-of-2-18-Billion/default.aspx) supplies the Yazoo scope and restart revision above; its restart timing includes the exact phrase “during the first half of 2027.”

- Atomic question: Does this candidate excerpt support LSB still producing the fertilizer-grade HDAN being proposed as a direct replacement? Expected label: **No**. Do not infer absence of AN-solution competition from this negative exact-grade label.

**Pair C — contracted output does not establish free replacement capacity.**

- Constraint source: the same CF revision as Pair B; do not count it as another independent event.
- Candidate source: [LSB 10-K filed February 27, 2025, recent-business-developments section](https://investors.lsbindustries.com/static-files/c65acd71-d1c1-436c-893d-3826c060cdd3) describes its Freeport ANS supply agreement: “for a five-year period commencing January 1, 2025, up to 150,000 short tons per year”. The surrounding paragraph says conventional ANS shipments from El Dorado began in early 2025. This source precedes both outage notices.
- Atomic question: Does this excerpt establish that the stated ANS quantity is uncommitted capacity available for replacement buyers? Expected label: **Unknown / not established**, with **contracted-supply evidence = Yes**. The phrase up to is a contractual ceiling, not a statement of full utilization or zero spare capacity. Code must not add this quantity to available supply.
