## What changed decisions

The primary question requires a comparable same-year aggregate front-end equipment plan and an explicitly supported upward revision. All 13 source references reject that evidentiary claim. Rejection means the supplied pair does not support it, not that equipment demand did not increase in reality.

- Jev with the registered 0.80 chosen-category probability threshold: 13 correct rejections, zero unsupported positives.
- The same Jev answers using categories alone: identical 13 rejections. **Zero primary decisions changed because of probabilities.**
- Conventional compact control: 10 correct rejections and three unsupported positives, in TSMC 2025 Q4, 2026 Q1 and 2026 Q2.
- Reviewed-number total-capex midpoint proxy: five correct rejections, three unsupported positives and five unresolved cases. This coarse proxy lacks composition information; it is not an automatic document parser.

The compact control conflated total capex, component, timing or cost statements with comparable aggregate equipment plans in those three cases. Jev identified the missing comparison basis. The registered engineering threshold of two additional correct primary decisions versus each control was met **only as a negative-claim filtering comparison**. An always-reject diagnostic also achieves 13/13 on this all-negative population; that observation is post-result context, not a new registered arm. Positive sensitivity is unmeasurable, and neither the threshold nor specificity establishes alpha.

Across 139 unambiguous atomic labels, Jev matched 121 (87.1%) and the compact control 78 (56.1%). Seventeen of 156 labels were excluded using the pre-call ambiguity mask. Event-macro agreement was 87.7% versus 55.7%; all-label secondary matches were 127/156 versus 87/156. These are agent-reviewed, dependent interpretation judgments on 13 episodes, not 139 independent trading observations, human gold labels or a financial holdout. This comparison does not establish superiority to all conventional models or prompting methods.

## Native uncertainty did not earn a financial role

The secondary non-front-end explanation route exposes the tradeoff. Raw frozen-label agreement was 6/13 for probability-routed Jev, 11/13 for categorical Jev and 12/13 for compact. Native routing changed six cases: one unsupported affirmative became unresolved, one frozen supported reference became unresolved, and four correct rejections became unresolved. The sole supported secondary reference depends on an explicitly ambiguous Micron FY2026 Q2 driver label and is **not a robust positive**.

A post-run reporting clarification propagated the already frozen ambiguity mask through each route without relabeling or rerunning models. It conservatively allows any registered category for a masked condition; an unambiguous false still rejects the route. All 13 primary references remain robust. Nine secondary references remain robust, all rejected: native Jev matches five, categorical Jev eight and compact eight. No robust positive sensitivity can be estimated for either route. Preserve the raw metrics alongside this clarification; do not present abstention alone as useful uncertainty calibration.
